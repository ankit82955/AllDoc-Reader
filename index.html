Privacy Policy

AllDoc Reader
Effective Date: September 18, 2026
Developer: AllDoc Reader Team
Offline-First Guarantee: AllDoc Reader does NOT collect, upload, store, or transmit any of your personal files, documents, or personal data to any external server. Everything stays 100% on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to AllDoc Reader ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines how AllDoc Reader handles data, device storage permissions, and third-party advertising services.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Document Uploads: When viewing, zooming, editing, drawing on, or saving documents (PDF, Word DOCX, Excel XLSX, PowerPoint PPTX, and TXT), all processing takes place entirely on your device's local CPU and storage. None of your document text, numbers, or slides are transmitted over the internet.
  • No Personal Accounts: You do not need an account, email address, or login to use the app.
  • No Precise Location Tracking: We never access or collect your device's GPS or precise location.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: We do not access your contacts, microphone, camera, or call logs.

3. Device Permissions and Usage

To function as a universal document reader and editor, AllDoc Reader requests access to device storage:

Permission Purpose
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Media Permissions Used exclusively to discover, list, and open user-selected documents from your device storage.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Storage Management Used strictly to save user-edited document modifications, annotations, and ink drawings back to the file on your device.
Document Intent ("Open With") Allows your operating system to launch AllDoc Reader when opening files from WhatsApp, Gmail, Telegram, or File Manager.

4. In-Place File Editing & Overwriting

AllDoc Reader allows in-place document editing. When you tap "Save" in any document viewer, the changes are saved directly back to the original file path on your device. No duplicate copies or external cloud backups are created by our application.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

AllDoc Reader may display third-party advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC) to keep our core features free for everyone. Google AdMob and its partners may collect pseudonymous identifiers to provide relevant advertisements, such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID)
  • Device model, OS version, and language settings
  • Coarse technical diagnostics

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy

You can opt out of interest-based ads anytime in your Android phone settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or Delete Advertising ID.

6. Children's Privacy

AllDoc Reader does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Security

Because AllDoc Reader operates offline and does not upload your files, your documents remain safely stored within your device's secured sandbox storage.
